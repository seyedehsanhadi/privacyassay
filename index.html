How the score works

Each reading is your real value (shown), a value everyone on your browser shares (blended), or nothing (refused). Only shown readings help a tracker.

The score is the share of what could identify you that stays hidden, weighted by how much each reading gives you away. A strong reading still shown is named next to the grade.

A high score means the fingerprint is hidden, not that you are untrackable: your IP and TLS are sent before any script. Blended is credited only for values a browser is documented to share. Method and working in METHODOLOGY.md.
